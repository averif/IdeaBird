Privacy policy

What IdeaBird collects, why, who handles it for us, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

Last updated · October 31, 2026 (billing added)

The short version

  • 01

    Your screenshots are analyzed and not stored. Your drafts are private to your account.

  • 02

    Write Like Me reads your X posts read-only, keeps only a style profile, and never posts for you.

01

What we collect

  • Account details: your email, your name if you give it, and whether your email is verified. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash, never in readable form.
  • Google sign-in: if you use Google, we receive your Google account ID, email and name. We don't get access to your other Google data.
  • Sessions: for each signed-in device we keep a hashed session token, your browser's user agent and your IP address. This keeps you signed in and helps us spot misuse.
  • Your history: a short summary of each screenshot or link (for links, also its address and title) and the drafts IdeaBird wrote for it.
  • Write Like Me, only if you connect X: see section 03.
  • Usage counts: how many posts you generated this month, so we can apply your plan's limit, plus short-lived counters per account, IP address or email for rate limits and to block repeated failed logins.
  • Billing (only if you subscribe to Pro): your plan, billing period, subscription status, renewal date, and the customer and subscription IDs from our payment provider. We never see or store your card or bank details; the payment provider handles them.

02

Screenshots and drafts

  • When you upload a screenshot, it's sent to our AI provider (NVIDIA) to be analyzed, then discarded. We don't store the image.
  • When you paste a link, our server fetches that public page (for a YouTube video: its captions and description) and sends the text to our AI provider to be analyzed, then discards it. The website you link to sees a request from our server, not from you. We don't store the page's content.
  • If small text is hard to read, IdeaBird may run text recognition in your browser. That text is sent along with the analysis and isn't stored on its own.
  • On Pro, the summary and the drafts are saved in your private history. Nobody else can see them, and you can delete any item from History. On Free, drafts aren't saved.
  • When you use Refine, the post you paste is sent to our AI provider to be edited and returned to you. We don't store the post or the refined version.

03

Write Like Me and your X account

Write Like Me is an optional Pro feature. When you connect X, IdeaBird learns how you write so new drafts sound like you.

  • Permissions: read-only. We ask X for tweet.read, users.read and offline.access; the last one means you don't have to reconnect every two hours. IdeaBird can't post, delete, like, follow, send messages or change anything on your account.
  • What we read: your X user ID and username, and up to about 80 of your recent original posts (no reposts or replies). We read them only when you connect and when you tap Refresh my voice. We never read them in the background or when you generate posts.
  • What we keep: a style profile (tone, sentence style, hooks, formatting, punctuation, emoji use and things you avoid) plus a few measured habits, such as typical post length. We don't store your posts. They're sent once to our AI provider to build the profile, then discarded. The profile describes how you write, never what you wrote, and we filter out anything copied from your posts.
  • Tokens: the access tokens X gives us are encrypted and stay on our servers. They're never sent to your browser or written to logs.
  • Who sees it: only you. Your profile shapes your own drafts only. It's never shared with other users or combined with anyone else's.
  • Disconnecting: Settings → Write Like Me → Disconnect revokes IdeaBird's access at X and deletes your tokens and voice profile. You can also remove IdeaBird from the connected apps in your X settings. If you connect a different X account, we delete the old profile.

We handle data from X in line with X's Developer Agreement and Policy.

04

How we use data

  • To run IdeaBird: sign you in, analyze screenshots, write drafts and show your history.
  • To send account emails: verification codes and password reset links. No marketing emails.
  • To run your subscription: apply your plan, show your billing status, and keep it in sync with the payment provider.
  • To keep IdeaBird safe: rate limits, blocking abuse, and detecting stolen sessions.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. We don't use your screenshots, drafts or X posts to train AI models.

05

Who processes it

We use a small number of service providers, and only to run IdeaBird:

  • NVIDIA (AI models) analyzes screenshots, writes drafts, refines posts you paste, and builds your voice profile from your posts.
  • Vercel (hosting) runs the app and gives us privacy-friendly, aggregated page analytics without advertising cookies.
  • Neon (database) stores your account, history, encrypted X tokens and voice profile.
  • Email delivery (Resend, or Google's Gmail) sends verification codes and reset links.
  • Dodo Payments (payments) processes Pro subscriptions as our merchant of record. It collects your payment details, billing address and email at checkout, charges you, handles taxes and invoices, and runs the billing portal. Its own privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Google, only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • X, only if you connect it for Write Like Me.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own.

06

Cookies

  • ib_rt keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Scripts on the page can't read it.
  • ib_xs lasts 10 minutes while you connect X. It makes sure the connection finishes in the same browser that started it.

That's all: no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

07

Retention and deletion

  • Screenshots, linked pages and X posts: not stored. They're discarded after processing.
  • History: kept until you delete it. If you move from Pro to Free, saved drafts stay in your account, hidden, and come back if you upgrade again.
  • Billing records: we keep your plan and subscription details while you have an account. Dodo Payments keeps payment and invoice records as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Voice profile and X tokens: kept until you disconnect X or delete your account.
  • Sessions: end after 30 days, or sooner when you log out or use Log out of all devices.
  • Verification codes and reset links: expire within minutes and work only once.
  • Your account: to delete it and everything linked to it, email us from the address on your account (see section 10). We'll delete it within 30 days.

08

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords and session tokens are stored as hashes, and X tokens are encrypted at rest. Every request is checked so you can only reach your own data. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password and log out of devices you no longer use.

09

Your choices

  • Delete drafts from History at any time.
  • Turn Write Like Me off, or disconnect X, from Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account. Depending on where you live, local law may give you more rights, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

IdeaBird isn't meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age for online services where you live.
