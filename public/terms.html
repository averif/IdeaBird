01

The service

IdeaBird turns screenshots you upload into social content drafts. An AI model looks at the screenshot, works out what it shows and what's worth saying about it, and writes draft posts for X and LinkedIn.

You are responsible for reviewing, editing, and verifying anything you publish.

02

Your account

You need an account to analyze screenshots and generate posts. You can sign up with email, which we confirm with a one-time code, or with Google.

  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for activity on your account.
  • If you think someone else has access, reset your password and use Log out of all devices from the account menu.
  • Usage is rate limited to keep the service available for everyone.

03

Content policy

We don't generate amplified versions of unverified political allegations or health misinformation.

IdeaBird may block generation when a screenshot appears to contain unverified allegations presented as fact, conspiracy claims, or content designed to spread misinformation.

04

Responsible use

  • Do not use IdeaBird to spread false or misleading claims.
  • Do not present generated drafts as verified reporting without independent verification.
  • Do not use generated content to harass, impersonate, or mislead people.
  • Only upload screenshots you have the right to use.

05

Generated drafts

Outputs are drafts, not endorsements or factual verification. AI can misread a screenshot or get details wrong. Always check names, dates, claims, numbers, and context before publishing.

06

Your screenshots and data

  • Screenshots are sent to our AI provider (NVIDIA) to be analyzed, then discarded. We don't store the images.
  • For each generation we save a short summary of what the screenshot showed and the drafts we wrote, in your private history. Only you can see it, and you can delete any item at any time.
  • We store your email, your name if you give it, and your password only as a secure hash, never in readable form.

Our Privacy policy has the details, including how Write Like Me uses your X account.

07

Changes

We may update these terms as IdeaBird changes. When we do, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use IdeaBird after a change means you accept the updated terms.
