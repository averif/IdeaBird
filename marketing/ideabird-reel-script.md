# IdeaBird reel: "Your camera roll is a graveyard"

**Length:** 15–18s · **Format:** 9:16, phone screen recording plus pixel-bird mascot overlay · **Style:** low-polish, first-person

**Strategy borrowed from the research:**
- Opal's self-confrontation number (the viewer checks their own screenshot count)
- Duolingo's guilt-tripping, slightly unhinged mascot
- Cal AI's speed demo
- Lovable's comment-keyword CTA

---

## Shot list

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Lever |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5s | Thumb opens Photos → *Screenshots* album. Counter: **4,812**. | **"go check how many screenshots you have. i'll wait."** | Self-confrontation, open loop |
| 1.5–3.5s | The pixel bird slides in from the edge, staring at the counter. Zoom punch on the number. | Bird: **"4,812 screenshots. 0 posts. we need to talk."** | Mascot guilt |
| 3.5–7.0s | Fast flicks through the screenshots (dashboard, tweet, chart, app UI). Each gets a red stamp: **SAVED TO POST LATER → NEVER POSTED**. | "saved it to post later" ×3, then "(you never did)" | Relatable humor, loss aversion |
| 7.0–11.5s | One screenshot is dragged into IdeaBird. A timer runs in the corner. Five post cards pop in: Hot take, Question, Stat-lead, Thread, LinkedIn. Timer stops at **0:07**. | **"7 seconds. 5 posts. from ONE screenshot."** | Speed demo, specific number |
| 11.5–14.0s | Bird hovers over the Copy button, menacing. | Bird: **"post one. or i start deleting your camera roll."** | Unhinged mascot threat |
| 14.0–16.0s | End card: logo, ideabird.yasirk.me | **"comment BIRD and i'll send you the link 🐦"** | Keyword CTA |

**Audio:** use a trending suspense or "dun dun" sound for 0–3.5s, a beat drop on the first NEVER POSTED stamp, and a cash-register *ding* when the timer stops. Add no voiceover, so the reel stays text-driven and works on mute, like the Duolingo and Opal reels.

## Caption

> be honest. how many screenshots are in your camera roll right now? 👇
>
> mine was 4,812 😭 IdeaBird turns any screenshot into 5 ready-to-post drafts in seconds.
>
> comment **BIRD** and i'll DM you the link (free to try)

The number question gets people commenting their counts, which boosts the reel in the algorithm. The keyword gets people commenting for the link. Set up an auto-DM (for example ManyChat) to send the link to everyone who comments **BIRD**.

## 3 alternative hooks to A/B test (same body)
1. **Fake confession (Cal AI):** "i need to come clean about my camera roll…"
2. **Mascot guilt (Duolingo):** bird holding a phone: "you screenshotted that chart 3 weeks ago. where's the post?"
3. **Big number (ChatGPT creators):** "i turned 1 screenshot into 5 viral posts in 7 seconds"

## Follow-up series (character continuity, like Duolingo)
- The bird reacts to a follower's screenshot count in the comments (reply-with-reel).
- "the bird reads your LinkedIn drafts" (roast format).
- Creator collab: a founder or marketer on camera says "my favorite hobby? feeding the bird" (Brick-style UGC).
